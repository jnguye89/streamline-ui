skriin ai tv: the two-way TV

skriin turns the TV you already own into a two-way TV. Watch live shows, streams and video podcasts from real people, go live from your couch with one button, record a video podcast and talk to your TV out loud.

Go live to skriin, YouTube, Twitch and Kick at the same time, with no streaming PC and no encoder settings. Watching is free in any browser, and you can start going live from your iPhone with the free app.
